# Code of conduct

Participants must treat each other respectfully, discuss technical disagreements constructively, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of private information. Critique ideas and behavior rather than personal attributes.

Maintainers may edit or remove violating content, limit participation, or exclude repeat or serious violators. Decisions should consider impact, context, and opportunities to correct behavior. Report concerns privately to the project owner using the contact in SECURITY.md. Do not publish sensitive reports in public issues.

This is a project-specific policy, not a claim of adherence to a third-party enforcement program.
